Drywall repair in Ulster County.

From nail pops and doorknob holes to crushed corners and peeling seams, I patch drywall so the damage disappears. Taped, mudded, sanded smooth and ready for paint.

What's included

What the job covers.

01

Holes and dents

Small holes, leftover anchors, doorknob dents and fist size holes patched flush with the wall.

02

Corners and seams

Cracked or crushed corner bead, peeling tape and seam cracks rebuilt straight and clean.

03

Larger patches

Damaged sections cut out and replaced with new drywall, then taped and finished.

04

Paint ready or painted

I can leave the repair primed and ready for paint, or paint it so it blends into the wall.

Questions

Will I be able to see the patch?

The goal is no. I feather the joint compound out wide and sand it smooth so the repair blends in once it's painted.

Do you take small patch jobs?

Yes. Small jobs are a big part of what I do. Bundle a few together to get the most out of a visit.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size and condition of the job. Use the rough price estimator for an instant range, then send a few photos for a free written quote.
