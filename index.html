02 · About

When you call me, you get me.

I grew up outside Cincinnati helping my dad, a landscape architect, on his jobs. Later I spent a few summers on my buddy's house painting crew, on homes and commercial buildings. That's where I learned what people expect when a crew pulls up. Show up on time, do the work right, and deal with the surprises without making them the client's problem.

After design school I spent about ten years as a graphic designer in New York, mostly behind a computer. When we moved to the Hudson Valley in 2022, I started fixing up our own house and remembered how much I like working with my hands. It's hard to beat taking something in rough shape and making it nice again.

I live in Wallkill with my first kid. Where I grew up there weren't any mountains, so I still look for Minnewaska and Awosting every time I drive down 208.

    Questions

    Do you give free estimates?

    Yes. Send photos for a quick ballpark, or I'll come out for larger jobs and give you a written quote.

    Do you do small jobs?

    Yes. Small jobs are a big part of what I do. Bundle a few together to get the most out of a visit.

    Do you handle permits for fences?

    Yes. I'll check your town's requirements and pull permits when they're needed.

    Do you do electrical or plumbing?

    No. Those need licensed trades, and I'm happy to refer you to someone I trust.

    What areas do you serve?

    Anywhere within a 45 minute drive of Wallkill in Ulster and Orange County, NY. That includes Kingston, New Paltz, Gardiner, Highland, Rosendale, Stone Ridge, Kerhonkson and Ellenville in Ulster County, and Walden, Montgomery, Pine Bush, Newburgh, Cornwall, Washingtonville, Middletown, Goshen, Chester and Monroe in Orange County.

    How do I pay?

    Card, check, cash, Venmo or Zelle. Jobs over $500 need a deposit to schedule.
