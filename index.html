03 · About

When you call me, you get me.

I'm Kenny, and I've been making things my whole life. I grew up working alongside my dad, a landscape architect, and did that work with him right up until college. After design school I spent ten years as a designer in New York City.

When I moved to the Hudson Valley in 2022, I started taking on more and more renovation projects around the house. It turned out I loved the work, so now I'm offering it for hire. You get a designer's eye and a builder's care on every job, big or small.

I started Phillips Handyman & Home Services because I saw how hard it is to find someone reliable for the jobs that are too small for a big contractor but too big to do yourself. I return calls, show up on time, give you a fair written price, and leave your place cleaner than I found it.

I live in Wallkill and work across Ulster County. When I'm not on a job, I'm at home taking care of my first kid.

05 · FAQ

Straight answers, up front.

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. Send photos for a quick ballpark, or I'll come out for larger jobs and give you a written quote.

Do you do small jobs?

Yes. Small jobs are a big part of what I do. Bundle a few together to get the most out of a visit.

Do you handle permits for fences?

Yes. I'll check your town's requirements and pull permits when they're needed.

Do you do electrical or plumbing?

No. Those need licensed trades in Ulster County, and I'm happy to refer you to someone I trust.

What areas do you serve?

Southern and central Ulster County, NY, including Wallkill, New Paltz, Gardiner, Highland, Marlboro, Rosendale, Stone Ridge, Kerhonkson, Ellenville, Esopus, Port Ewen and Kingston.

How do I pay?

Card, check, cash, Venmo or Zelle. Jobs over $500 need a deposit to schedule.
