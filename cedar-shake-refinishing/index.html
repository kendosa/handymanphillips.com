Questions

Should I replace or refinish my cedar roof?

If shakes are cracked, cupped, mossy or soft, or you can see daylight through them, it's time for a new roof. If they're just gray and dry, cleaning and a fresh finish can buy years. Send me photos and I'll tell you honestly which one you need.

How do I know my cedar needs refinishing?

If it looks gray, dry or blotchy, or water soaks in instead of beading up, it's time. Send me photos and I'll let you know.

How much does a new cedar shake roof cost?

It depends on the size and pitch of the roof and what's under the old shakes. Send me photos and rough dimensions for a free written quote.
