What's included

What the job covers.

01

Microcement

A thin, seamless cement finish troweled on in layers and sealed. Smooth, durable and great over existing concrete.

02

Engineered hardwood

White oak and other engineered planks laid tight and straight, with expansion gaps hidden under trim.

03

Floor prep

Old flooring pulled up, subfloor screwed down, low spots leveled and the surface cleaned before anything new goes down.

04

Transitions and trim

Thresholds, reducers and new baseboard so every edge and doorway looks finished.

Questions

How long does a microcement floor take?

Usually a few days, since each coat needs time to dry before the next one and the sealer. I'll give you a clear schedule with your quote.

Can you put new flooring over my old floor?

Sometimes. It depends on what's there and how flat it is. Send me photos and I'll tell you whether it needs to come up first.

How much does new flooring cost?

It depends on the size of the room, the material and how much prep the subfloor needs. Send me photos and rough dimensions for a free written quote.
