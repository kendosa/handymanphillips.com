    Questions

    Do you give free estimates?

    Yes. Send photos for a quick ballpark, or I'll come out for larger jobs and give you a written quote.

    Do you do small jobs?

    Yes. Small jobs are a big part of what I do. Bundle a few together to get the most out of a visit.

    Do you handle permits for fences?

    Yes. I'll check your town's requirements and pull permits when they're needed.

    Do you do electrical or plumbing?

    No. Those need licensed trades, and I'm happy to refer you to someone I trust.

    What areas do you serve?

    Anywhere within a 45 minute drive of Wallkill in Ulster and Orange County, NY. That includes Kingston, New Paltz, Gardiner, Highland, Rosendale, Stone Ridge, Kerhonkson and Ellenville in Ulster County, and Walden, Montgomery, Pine Bush, Newburgh, Cornwall, Washingtonville, Middletown, Goshen, Chester and Monroe in Orange County.

    How do I pay?

    Card, check, cash, Venmo or Zelle. Jobs over $500 need a deposit to schedule.
